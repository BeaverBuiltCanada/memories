<br>Flattened steel barrels used as siding<br>
During the Gold Rush and the decades after, steel barrels were everywhere — shipped in with fuel, oil, food supplies, and mining chemicals. They piled up faster than anyone could use them. Lumber was expensive, winters were brutal, and people needed any material that could survive the freeze–thaw cycles.

So some buildings were patched, repaired, or fully sided with flattened‑out steel barrels.
It was cheap, tough, and already on hand.
