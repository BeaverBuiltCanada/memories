<h5>LABOUR FORCE</h5>
The dredge required a skilled labour force to operate and maintain its complex machinery. Workers lived in camps near the dredge site and performed a variety of tasks, including operating the winches, maintaining the bucket line, repairing equipment, and managing the flow of gravel and water through the system.
