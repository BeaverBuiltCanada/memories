<h5>Goldbottom Mine Tours — A Family in the Goldfields</h5>
Goldbottom Mine Tours is run by the Millar family, who have been working these creeks for generations. They started mining on Goldbottom and Hunker Creek back in the 1970s, living right on the claims and learning the trade the same way the old‑timers did — by doing it. Today, three generations of Millars still operate in the Klondike, running a working placer mine through the summer and sharing their way of life with visitors. It’s one of the few places where you can step onto an active claim and hear the mining story from people who’ve lived it their whole lives.
