<h5>Water, Gravity and Electricity</h5>
Everything depended on water.

All placer (loose) gold mining requires two things: water and gravity.

Inside the dredge, a large pipeline sprayed water into the trommel to wash the smaller and heavier gold-bearing gravel through holes into the sluice tables. More water washed over the sluice tables, carrying away all but the heaviest particles.

A hydro-electric plant 60 km away provided power to massive water pumps, winches and the bucket line.
