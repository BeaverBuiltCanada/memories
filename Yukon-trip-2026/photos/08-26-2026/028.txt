<h5>Canadian Klondyke Mining Company
Dredge No. 4</h5>
Historic Civil Engineering Site
Canadian Society for Civil Engineering

This dredge operated from 1912 to 1959. Designed to float and support 3,000 tons, it featured a chain of large iron excavating buckets. It produced 300,000 ounces of gold and mined 65 million cubic yards of gravel. The site also included hydroelectric, water supply, and machine shop facilities.
