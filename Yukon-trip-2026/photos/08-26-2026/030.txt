<h5>BIG BUSINESS</h5>
By the 1930s, gold mining in the Klondike had become big business. Large companies invested heavily in dredges, power plants, machine shops, and transportation systems. These operations required enormous capital and employed hundreds of workers to keep the dredges running around the clock.
