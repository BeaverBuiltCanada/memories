<h5>The River Queen</h5>
was a beloved paddle‑wheel tour boat that operated for decades on Yellowknife Bay, carrying visitors across the calm waters of Great Slave Lake. Built in the classic riverboat style, she became one of Yellowknife’s most recognizable summer attractions. Sightseeing cruises offered slow, scenic trips past rocky shorelines, houseboats, and northern sunsets. As tourism changed and operating costs rose, the River Queen eventually shut down, leaving behind memories of warm evenings on the water and the gentle churn of her paddle wheel. Today, she’s remembered as a small but iconic piece of Yellowknife’s history.
