<h5>Miles Canyon basalts</h5>

Volcanoes in Whitehorse?  
Beginning around 8.4 million years ago, lava flowed from a series of volcanoes that erupted near Whitehorse. The lava spread across the landscape, filling valleys and creating a broad, flat surface. Over time, the lava cooled and hardened into basalt rock. Later, glaciers carved through the basalt, forming the steep canyon walls seen today. The Miles Canyon basalts are evidence of this ancient volcanic activity.
