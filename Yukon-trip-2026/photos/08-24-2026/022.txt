The Cremation of Sam McGee — Summary  
Robert W. Service’s famous Yukon ballad tells the story of Sam McGee, a Southerner who could never get warm in the northern winter. As he freezes on a brutal trail near Lake Lebarge, he makes his partner promise to cremate him if he dies. The partner reluctantly agrees, and after Sam passes, he hauls the body across the icy wilderness until he finds an abandoned barge. He builds a roaring fire in the furnace and carries out the promise — only to open the door and find Sam sitting inside, warm and cheerful at last.
