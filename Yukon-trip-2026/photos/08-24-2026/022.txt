A famous Yukon tale about a man named Sam McGee, who could never escape the cold. 
On a brutal winter journey, he made his partner promise to cremate him if he died. 
The poem follows that promise across frozen trails, ending with the twist that Sam 
is found smiling inside the furnace, finally warm for the first time since Tennessee.
