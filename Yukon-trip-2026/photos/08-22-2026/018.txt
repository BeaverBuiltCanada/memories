Journey From Here — An Adventure Awaits  
You’re standing at the 60th parallel, the border between British Columbia and Yukon. From here, the highway opens into a northern landscape shaped by rivers, lakes, mountains, and long stretches of wilderness. This region offers campgrounds, recreation sites, and historic travel routes that connect communities across both territories. Whether you head north into Yukon or south toward British Columbia, the road ahead leads to wide‑open spaces, quiet waters, and the kind of adventure only the North can offer.
