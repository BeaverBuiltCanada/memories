North of 60°  
The 60th parallel is an imaginary east‑west line that circles the Earth and marks the northern boundary of British Columbia and the southern boundary of Yukon. Crossing this latitude means entering a region defined by long summer days, short winter daylight, and a northern climate that shapes both landscape and culture.

Who shares the 60th parallel?  
This latitude runs across the northern hemisphere, touching parts of Canada, Norway, Sweden, Finland, and Russia. Communities along 60° north experience similar daylight extremes and seasonal rhythms despite being spread across continents.

How much light is there?  
At 60° north, the sun stays above the horizon for nearly 19 hours during the summer solstice, while winter brings less than six hours of daylight. Cold temperatures can reach levels where boiling water thrown into the air freezes instantly.
