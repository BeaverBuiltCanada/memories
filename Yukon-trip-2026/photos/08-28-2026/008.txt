<h5>It looks like a desert</h5>
<h5>… but it is really the remains of an ancient lake</h5>

About 10,000 years ago, this area was covered by a large glacial lake. As the glaciers melted, sand and silt were left behind. Winds from the Watson River continue to move the sand, creating the dunes you see today.
