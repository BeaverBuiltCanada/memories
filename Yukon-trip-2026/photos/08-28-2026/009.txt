<h5>TODAY – An Ancient but Active Environment</h5>

Wind continues to move sand across the dunes, reshaping them year after year.
Glacial deposits, old lake sediments, and modern wind‑blown sands all combine to form this constantly changing landscape.

The Carcross dunes are one of the few places in the Yukon where you can see these ancient processes still at work today.
