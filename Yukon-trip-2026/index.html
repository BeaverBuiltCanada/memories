dedicated in loving memory of my Grandfather
William James Fitzgerald
aka: Bill — Grandpa

We always loved spending time together, ever since I was a little kid. I am so greatful for the time we got to share when I brought Grandpa to Edmonton on the train.

We had planned on taking a trip to the Yukon when I turned 16. Unfortunately we were never able to take it. I was finally able to take it with my own family. This journey was meant to be shared — mile by mile, story by story. Every stop, every sign, every photo is a piece of the road we imagined together.

And so… The Great Yukon 2026 Adventure begins
